Poster Instructions

Workshops: 61 cm (W) × 85 cm (H) Portrait format. Authors are NOT permitted to hang any posters on the walls of the venue, including in your workshop room. There will be allocated poster boards near your workshop room. Use only the venue approved command strips to hang your poster. These will be in your workshop room for authors to pick up. Please only use one strip per corner of your poster. Please take your poster down after your poster session as many workshops have multiple sessions in one day. Please refer to the NeurIPS poster instructions for further details: https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026/PosterInstructions. Feel free to contact us at roco-spring-org@googlegroups.com with any questions.
