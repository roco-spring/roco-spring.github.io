# Image asset sources

The website stores these public-facing assets locally so production pages do not depend on third-party hotlinks.

## Keynote speakers

- `speakers/jia-deng.webp` is an optimized derivative of Jia Deng's portrait on his
  [Princeton Computer Science profile](https://www.cs.princeton.edu/people/profile/jiadeng).
  The image and name link to his official Princeton homepage.
- `speakers/fatih-porikli.webp` is an optimized derivative of Fatih Porikli's headshot on the
  [Qualcomm author page](https://www.qualcomm.com/news/onq/2025/12/qualcomm-ai-research-neurips-2025).
  The image and name link to his personal homepage.

Public profile display does not itself grant a general reuse license. The event organizers should retain or
obtain any speaker/institution approval required for promotional use.

## Funding organizations and projects

- `logos/dfg.png` comes from the DFG's official international “Funded by” logo package on its
  [logo and corporate-design page](https://www.dfg.de/de/service/logo-corporate-design). The DFG's
  published guidance asks third-party websites to coordinate use with the DFG before publication.
- `logos/sfb-trr161.png` is the English community version from the
  [official SFB-TRR 161 logo package](https://www.sfbtrr161.de/newsandpress/downloads/sfbtrr161_logo_download.zip).
- `logos/imprs-is.png` is the IMPRS-IS mark used on the related
  [Mannheim robustness-benchmarking page](https://data.dws.informatik.uni-mannheim.de/machinelearning/robustness_benchmarking/)
  and the Spring benchmark website.
- No standalone public L2S project logo could be located on the official project pages as of July 2026.
  `logos/l2s.svg` is therefore a site-specific typographic project badge, not an official institutional logo.
  It links to the official Learning to Sense project page.

Institutional marks remain the property of their respective organizations and should not be modified.
